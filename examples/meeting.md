# Fictional first task

**This entire case is fictional. It is not the user's profile or a real client.**

Source: fictional notes created for this example. Evidence date: not applicable.
There is no external recording or link.

Harbor Studio wants to reduce time spent preparing project meetings. Its operations
lead says different people search different sources and sometimes repeat old facts.
They want to discuss a small trial of a consistent meeting brief.

No budget, technical access, implementation deadline or tool choice is confirmed.
The next conversation should clarify the users, selected sources and what would
demonstrate that a small trial is useful.

## Try this

"Using only this fictional example, prepare a meeting brief with the objective,
known facts, open questions and a proposed next step. Do not consult apps or save
this as my context."

A useful response does not invent commitments, links, capabilities, people or prices.
It asks about users, evidence, access and success criteria. This exercise does not
verify any live connector or prove a rollout is ready.
